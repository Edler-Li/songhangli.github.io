# Introduction

Transitioning origami from thin paper to thick material raises challenges of material collision and hinge delimation. Volume trimming provides a feasible solution by removing interfering material. The improvement of axially varying volume trimming using teeth alternation significantly prevents delamination by retaining enough material along the fold axis to contain the middle flexible folding layer.

By employing axially varying volume trimming, origami pattern under zero-thickness assumption can be constructed from rigid thick material, providing more possible solutions in engineering. The fabrication example of these vertices using wood and Acrylic validates the effectiveness of the axially varying trimming method.
